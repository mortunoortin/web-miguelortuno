About

I am a professor of physics at the Universidad de Murcia, where I work in the Department of Physics and the Centro de Investigación en Óptica y Nanofísica (CIOyN). My work combines experiment and computation in condensed matter physics, with a long-standing focus on how disorder shapes the electronic properties of solids.

Research

Disordered systems and quantum transport

Electron transport in strongly disordered materials, where interference and interactions compete with randomness. This includes hopping conduction, Anderson localization, and the collective effects that emerge when Coulomb interactions cannot be neglected.

Superconductor–insulator transitions in amorphous thin films

Experimental and numerical study of the transition between superconducting and insulating behaviour in amorphous films, a setting where a single tuning parameter drives a system across a genuine quantum phase transition.

Quantum information and computation

Work at the interface between many-body physics and quantum information, including numerical approaches to correlated quantum systems.
